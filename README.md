# Maker Badges

[![License: CC BY-SA 4.0](https://licensebuttons.net/l/by-sa/4.0/80x15.png)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/)

**Show off your maker skills!** Inspired by Scouting America's [Merit Badges](https://www.scouting.org/skills/merit-badges/), Maker Badges are physical badges you can create to celebrate the skills you and your fellow makers have earned.

## What Are Maker Badges?

Maker Badges recognize hands-on skills — from 3D printing and soldering to laser cutting and coding. Download the SVG designs from this repo and turn them into real, physical badges you can wear, display, or share.

## How to Use Them

- **Vinyl cut (recommended)** — These designs work best as vinyl cut stickers. Slap them on your laptop, car, toolbox, or water bottle.
- **3D print** — Print the designs as medallions or patches with a 3D printer.
- **Give them out** — Award badges to friends and fellow makers as they complete projects and level up their skills.
- **Make your own** — Have a skill that's not listed? Design a new badge and submit a pull request!
